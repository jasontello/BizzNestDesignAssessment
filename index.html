Who We Are

Open Source San José is a nonprofit civic innovation community where volunteers use technology, UX research, UI design, and partnerships to solve local problems.

Our Mission

We make community services more transparent, accessible, and equitable by building civic projects with local government and community-based organizations.

Our Vision

We aim to be a trusted technical partner for San José, nearby governments, and local nonprofits whenever community challenges need thoughtful digital solutions.

What We Do

We host bi-monthly civic hack nights where anyone can get onboarded, join a project, and contribute skills in code, design, research, organizing, writing, data, or subject-matter expertise.

Open Source San José typically meets the fourth Thursday of the month at 6:30 p.m. at Mountain Mikes Pizza, 29 S Third St, San Jose, CA 95113. Individual project teams may meet more regularly at other times.

Anyone can join! Technical skills are not required to contribute. We onboard new members at every meeting.
